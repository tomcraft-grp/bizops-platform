# Accounting Reconciliation Matrix

Version 1.0 · Addendum §23.7, §23.12, §23.13. Every subledger must reconcile to its general-ledger control account. The matrix is computed live by `reconciliationMatrix()` (screen **Accounting → Reconciliation**, with drill-down to the documents behind a difference) and re-checked by `runIntegrityChecks()` (ids `recon-*`) on every test that changes state.

**Rule:** any unexplained difference is a release-blocking exception for the affected workflow — never netted away, never posted to suspense.

## 1. Definitions
| Control (GL) | Accounts | Subledger / source of truth | Tolerance | Test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Customer receivables | `ar` role (1130) | Σ open order balances per customer (invoice − allocations); statement identity *opening + charges − payments − credits = closing* | 0.01 | `every control account agrees with its subledger…`, `customer statement obeys…` |
| Platform receivables | `platform_ar` (1120) | Σ unsettled platform orders (gross − settled) | 0.01 | same; `settlements: …platform receivable clears` |
| Supplier payables | `ap` (2100) | Σ open supplier invoices (goods receipts − payments − returns) | 0.01 | same |
| Inventory | `inv_materials` + `inv_goods` + `inv_transit` | Stock ledger value (Σ movements × cost) + in-transit | 0.01 | `inventory roll-forward ties to the GL`, `200 random stock operations…` |
| Customer credit | `customer_credit` (2300) | Σ unapplied receipts (overpayments, credit kept) | 0.01 | same |
| Supplier deposits | `supplier_deposit` (1140) | Σ deposit balances | 0.01 | same |
| VAT | `input_vat` + `output_vat` | Tax base of sales, purchases and expenses × effective rates | 0.01 | `VAT report: output − input…` |
| Cash & bank | `cash` + `bank` | Σ journal lines **ticked as matched to the bank/cash statement** | — | status is `pending` until lines are matched by a person |

Cross-statement identities (all asserted): **TB = Balance Sheet = P&L** (assets = liabilities + equity + current earnings; contra accounts carried with the right sign), **cash-flow opening + movements = closing** (transfers net to zero), **P&L by branch dimension sums to the all-branch P&L**, **Yesterday → Today → Now** carries balances with no unexplained difference for every control family over 14 consecutive days.

A "stray journal" on a control account (a manual or unmapped posting that has no subledger document) is *detected, not hidden* (test: `a stray journal on a control account is detected, not hidden`).

## 3. Procedure on a difference
1. Open *Reconciliation*, click the row to drill into the documents.
2. Classify: duplicate posting · missing posting · wrong mapping · timing (period) · manual journal on a control account · rounding.
3. Fix only by reversal + correct posting (four-eyes) — see `qa/INCIDENT_RUNBOOK.md` §4.1.
4. Re-run the matrix and the ACC suite; attach the result to the correction record.
